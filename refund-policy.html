Refund Policy.

Last updated: August 31, 2026

Project deposits

Project deposits reserve planning and production time. Because work begins after approval, deposits are normally non-refundable unless a written project agreement says otherwise.

Milestone work

For milestone-based projects, completed and approved work is payable according to the agreed scope. If a project is paused or cancelled, payment may be due for work completed up to that point.

Digital services

Design, development, consulting, maintenance and custom software are tailored services. Refund eligibility depends on the project agreement, completed work and any applicable law.

How to raise a concern

If you are unhappy with a deliverable, contact us promptly with clear details. We will review the agreed scope and work with you toward a reasonable resolution.
