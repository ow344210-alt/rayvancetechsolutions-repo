Product planning

Website or web app?

Choose based on the job your users need to complete.

Choose a website when your main goal is communication

A website is best for explaining your services, building trust, showing portfolio work and helping customers contact you. Most business websites do not require users to log in.

Choose a web app when users must complete repeat tasks

A web app is useful when users need accounts, dashboards, saved data, bookings, messaging, reports or a repeatable workflow. It is built around interaction rather than only information.

Start with the smallest useful version

Do not begin by building every possible feature. Identify the one task that saves time, reduces confusion or improves service for your users, then build that first.

Plan for growth

Decide who will use the product, what data it needs and which systems it must connect with. A clear first version makes future improvements simpler and more affordable.
