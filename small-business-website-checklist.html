Website launch

Small-business website checklist.

Check the essentials before sending customers to a new website.

1. Make your offer clear

A visitor should understand what you do, who you help and why they should choose you within a few seconds. Use simple headings and avoid vague marketing phrases.

2. Add one clear next step

Every important page should guide visitors to call, message, request a quote or book a consultation. Keep forms short and make your phone and WhatsApp options easy to find.

3. Check mobile design

Many visitors arrive from a phone. Test the menu, buttons, forms, text size and images on a real mobile device before launch.

4. Check trust details

Use your real business name, email, phone number, service area, portfolio examples and approved reviews. Consistent details help customers and Google understand your business.

5. Prepare for Google

Add page titles, descriptions, a sitemap, Search Console and a Google Business Profile. These steps help Google discover and understand your site after launch.
