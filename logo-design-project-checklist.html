Branding

Prepare for a logo design project.

Good brand design begins with clear business context.

Know who you want to reach

Describe your ideal customers, what they value and the feeling you want your business to create. A logo should be designed for the people you need to connect with.

Define your business goal

Are you launching, repositioning or making an existing business look more professional? The answer helps guide the visual direction.

Collect useful references

Share examples of brands you like and explain why. Also identify competitors so the final identity can feel distinct in your market.

List practical uses

Your logo may appear on social profiles, a website, invoices, signs, packaging or uniforms. Planning these uses helps ensure the design remains clear at every size.

Keep the brief focused

Include your name, services, audience, values, preferred tone and launch timeline. A focused brief makes feedback faster and decisions easier.
